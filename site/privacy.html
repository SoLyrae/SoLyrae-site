What is shared with others

When you open a page, your browser connects to services that provide the data and the fonts. Like any website, this means they can see your IP address.

  • Stakewiz and Vortex provide the validator data shown on the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces.
  • Validator logos in the explorer are loaded from the web addresses that each validator publishes.
  • Links to other sites, such as Sanctum, Jito or Solana explorers, are governed by their own policies.

What you type into the search box or the tools stays in your browser. We do not send it to anyone.
