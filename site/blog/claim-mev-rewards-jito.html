How to claim your MEV rewards from Jito

Guide · 13 Nov 2026 · SoLyrae

When you stake SOL natively, two kinds of rewards can come from your validator. Inflation rewards are added to your stake account automatically. MEV rewards are different: they are tracked for you every epoch, but you have to claim them. This guide explains how.

Do you need to do this?

  • Native stake with a validator that runs the Jito client: yes, MEV rewards exist for you and are claimed separately.
  • Native stake with a validator that does not run Jito: there are no MEV rewards to claim.
  • A liquid staking token such as JitoSOL: no. The token already includes MEV rewards.

You can check whether a validator runs Jito, and what MEV commission it charges, in the explorer. The column shows "–" for validators without Jito.

Steps

  1. Open Jito's harvest page, on Jito's official website, and connect the wallet that owns your stake accounts. Make sure you are on the real Jito address.
  2. The page lists your stake accounts and the unclaimed MEV rewards of each one.
  3. Turn on the accounts you want to claim from.
  4. Choose how to receive the rewards: as SOL, or converted to JitoSOL.
  5. Confirm and approve the transactions in your wallet. If you claim from several accounts, your wallet may ask you to approve more than one transaction.

Good to know

  • Claim when it is worth it. Each claim costs a small network fee. Jito's guide suggests claiming when the amount is larger than that cost.
  • Nothing to claim? Your validator may not run the Jito client, or the rewards may be too recent to show.
  • It is the same stake account. Claiming does not move or change your stake.
  • Check the process on Jito's own pages. Wallet screens and Jito's tools change from time to time.

Where to see the numbers

On our rewards page you can see the MEV tips paid to the stakers of SoLyrae in every epoch, next to the inflation rewards. For how both kinds of rewards come about, read where staking rewards come from.
