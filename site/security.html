How SoLyrae protects your stake

Three things matter: who controls your SOL, who controls the validator's keys, and what happens if the server fails.

Your SOL is yours

When you delegate, your SOL stays in a stake account that only you control. You hold its withdraw authority, so SoLyrae cannot move, spend or withdraw it.

A multisig controls the validator

The withdraw authority of SoLyrae's vote account, the key that controls the validator's own rewards and settings, is a multisig. Changes need more than one approval, so no single key can move them.

A backup server is ready

SoLyrae has a backup server, so the validator can keep voting if the main one fails. You can follow how the node behaves on the infrastructure page.

What we will never do

  • Ask for your recovery phrase or private keys.
  • Ask you to send SOL to us in order to stake.
  • Ask you to approve a transaction we cannot explain.

Staking involves risk, and no setup removes it. Read the risks of staking SOL before you delegate.
