What is the Solana Foundation Delegation Program?

Network · 13 Nov 2026 · SoLyrae

You will see "SFDP" among the sources of stake of many validators. It stands for the Solana Foundation Delegation Program. Here is what it is and what it means for you.

What it is

The Solana Foundation holds SOL and delegates part of it to validators that meet certain conditions. The goal is to support a healthy, decentralized network of independent validators. It is a delegation of stake, not a grant of money: the Foundation's SOL stays the Foundation's.

How the stake is shared out

According to the Foundation's published description, there are two parts:

  • Stake matching. The program matches part of the stake a validator gets from outside the program, up to a cap.
  • Residual stake. What is left is divided among the validators that meet the performance requirements.

The ratios and caps have changed over time, so check the Foundation's page for the current ones.

What validators must meet

The Foundation publishes its delegation criteria. They cover, among other things:

  • Technical performance, such as voting well.
  • A limit on commission.
  • Minimum software versions.
  • Limits on concentration, so that validators do not all depend on the same hosting provider.
  • A well-performing node on the test network as well.
  • Identity checks on the operator.

Participation lasts for a limited period and has to be renewed, and validators that stop meeting the criteria can lose it.

What it means for you as a delegator

  • It does not take anything from you. Foundation stake is separate from yours, and your rewards are calculated in the same way.
  • It is a signal, not a guarantee. It means the validator met the criteria when it was reviewed. It says nothing about future performance, and it is not an endorsement.
  • It affects where a validator's stake comes from. Our staking page shows, for SoLyrae, how much of the stake comes from the program and how much from liquid staking pools and other wallets. A validator that depends on the program for most of its stake relies on keeping its place in it.

Where to read the official rules

The criteria, the FAQ and the agreement are published by the Solana Foundation on its website, and they are updated over time. Always read the current version there, because secondary articles, this one included, can be out of date.
