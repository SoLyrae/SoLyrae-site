Vote account, identity and stake account: which address is which?

Guide · 13 Nov 2026 · SoLyrae

Staking on Solana involves several long addresses that look alike. Mixing them up is the most common reason people feel unsure, so here is what each one is.

The addresses of a validator

  • Vote account. This is the address you delegate to. It is the validator's public identity for staking, it is used to look the validator up, and it cannot be changed after it is created. SoLyrae's vote account is DPhzpiNGU9C6576uLsNSHmdi2AxwxpjMsRdh2iVC4TPh.
  • Identity account. The key the validator machine uses to sign its votes and to identify itself on the network. You do not delegate to it.
  • Withdraw authority of the vote account. The key that controls the validator's own rewards and settings. It is held by the operator and should be kept off the validator machine.

You can see SoLyrae's vote account and identity, with copy buttons, on the infrastructure page. When your wallet asks for a validator, always use the vote account.

Your stake account

When you delegate, your wallet creates a stake account that holds your staked SOL. It has its own address, and it has two keys that you control:

  • Stake authority. Allows delegating, deactivating, splitting and merging the stake account.
  • Withdraw authority. Allows withdrawing the SOL once it is not delegated, and can reset the stake authority. Wallets usually set both to your own address.

The validator holds neither of them. That is why a validator cannot move or spend your SOL.

Useful things you can do with a stake account

  • Split it into two stake accounts, for example to delegate part of it to another validator. The new account must hold at least the minimum delegation and cover its own small reserve.
  • Merge two stake accounts that share the same authorities.
  • Deactivate and withdraw when you want your SOL back.

Every stake account also keeps a small reserve, so the account can exist on the network. It is about 0.002 SOL and comes back when you withdraw everything and the account closes.

A quick way to check you are doing it right

  1. The validator address you paste is a vote account, and it matches the one published on the validator's own website.
  2. You can find that vote account in the explorer with the name you expect.
  3. Your wallet shows the stake account as yours, with you as the authority.
