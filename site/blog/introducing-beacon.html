Introducing Beacon: a free security bulletin for Solana validator operators

Network · Nov 14, 2026 · SoLyrae

A validator is only as safe as the software under it. A flaw can sit in the client, in a Rust library inside the client, in the Linux kernel or in a tool such as OpenSSH, and the news about it is scattered across many places. Beacon puts it in one.

What Beacon is

Beacon is a security bulletin for the people who run Solana validators. It is free, it is open to every operator and it is updated automatically. It lists vulnerabilities and security releases that matter to a validator, ranks them, and says what they affect and what to do.

What it covers

  • The validator clients: Agave, Jito and Firedancer, with their latest releases next to the minimum version the Solana Foundation's delegation program asks for.
  • The Rust crates inside them. Beacon reads the exact list of crates in the latest Agave and Jito releases and checks it against known advisories.
  • The operating system: the Linux kernel, Ubuntu and Debian notices.
  • The tools around a validator: OpenSSL, OpenSSH, systemd and Docker.
  • Known exploited vulnerabilities from the US cyber agency's public list, which is the clearest signal that something is being used in attacks.

How it ranks alerts

Each alert gets one of four levels. Critical means the flaw is known to be exploited. High means a severe rating or a high chance of exploitation. Watch means worth planning for, and Info is context. The ranking combines the severity score, whether the flaw is exploited and a public estimate of how likely exploitation is. Automatic alerts are never edited by hand. Urgent official notices to operators, which are often shared in Discord rather than in a public feed, are added from the original announcement, and Beacon then follows the patch status on Ubuntu and Debian.

Follow only what you run

Not every operator needs every alert. On the Beacon page you choose what you run, for example Agave and the Linux kernel, and you get a feed for exactly that. You can add it to any feed reader or to a chat tool that reads feeds, or sign up for a daily email with the new alerts for the parts you picked.

What Beacon is not

Beacon does not replace Anza's private alerts. Serious flaws in the clients are handled privately until a fix is ready, and Anza warns validators through its own channel. Beacon shows what is already public. It is a community service by SoLyrae and not an official channel of Solana, Anza, Jito or Firedancer, so always confirm in the linked source before you act.
