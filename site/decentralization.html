How we calculate it

We take the active stake of every validator (Stakewiz) and each validator's hosting provider, country and client (Vortex, last completed epoch), group it by the entity we are measuring (validator, hosting provider, country or client), sort from largest to smallest and count how many it takes to pass one third of total stake. Other trackers group providers and clients differently, so their numbers can differ. Read the longer explanation in our article.
