03 · Stake

Delegate in three steps

Staking is non-custodial: your SOL stays in a stake account that only you control, and you can unstake at any time.

  1. Open your wallet's staking screenPhantom, Solflare, Backpack, Ledger and most Solana wallets include a Stake option.
  2. Choose SoLyraeSearch for “SoLyrae” or paste the vote account on this page to be sure it is the right validator.
  3. Confirm the amountRewards are credited every epoch, about once a day. Keep a little SOL free for fees.

06 · FAQ

Common questions

Is my SOL safe when I delegate?

Delegating does not transfer your SOL. It stays in your own stake account and only you hold the withdraw authority; SoLyrae cannot move it. Staking still carries network and market risks.

How long does unstaking take?

Unstaking takes effect at the end of the current epoch, which is at most about a day away. After that the SOL is available to withdraw.

What does SoLyrae charge?

The explorer shows our commission straight from the chain, on rewards and on MEV, next to every other validator's.

Where does the data come from?

Validators, stake and APY from the public Stakewiz API; epoch history, rewards and each validator's hosting provider, country and client from the public Vortex API, refreshed every few minutes. If a source is down, the page says so instead of showing old numbers as live.
