How SoLyrae started

It began with Tour de Sun '22

SoLyrae started from the Solana Foundation's Tour de Sun '22 initiative. The program encouraged people to run validators in a wide range of data centers around the world, to make Solana more diverse in where and how it runs. The Foundation closed the program on 31 March 2024, and you can read its announcement here.

Live since 2 April 2024

SoLyrae went live on mainnet on 2 April 2024, and it has kept validating ever since. You can follow how it runs on the infrastructure page.

SoLyraeSOL, our liquid staking token

On 11 February 2025 SoLyrae launched SoLyrae Staked SOL (SoLyraeSOL), a liquid staking token for the SoLyrae validator, created with Sanctum. It gives people another way to stake with us: instead of delegating a stake account, you hold a token that represents your SOL staked through the pool. Read about the difference in native or liquid staking, and see the token on Sanctum. To delegate directly instead, go to Stake with us.

Why this website exists

Staking works best when people can check what they are delegating to. We built this site so that anyone can see how SoLyrae performs, compare it with every other validator on the network and follow the numbers from public sources, without having to take our word for it. Browse the performance, the explorer and the blog, or get in touch through the contact page.
