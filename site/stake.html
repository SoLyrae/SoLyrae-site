Rewards and unstaking

What to expect

Rewards

Credited at each epoch boundary (about once a day) into your stake account. They increase your stake automatically. The size depends on network inflation, how the validator performs and its commission.

Unstaking

Choose unstake (deactivate) in your wallet. The stake becomes inactive at the end of the current epoch, at most about a day away. Then withdraw it to your wallet balance.

Your control

You hold the withdraw authority of your stake account, so SoLyrae cannot move or spend your SOL. You can change validator or split your stake across several at any time.

Staking involves risk and rewards are not guaranteed. New to Solana? Follow the step-by-step guide, from creating a wallet, and read the FAQ before you start. Not financial advice.
